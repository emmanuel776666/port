Before you write

What to include for a fast, accurate quote

The more of these you can answer, the faster I can give you a real number instead of a vague estimate.

What the site must do

Show pages, sell a product, take bookings, let people sign in — or just look professional. The purpose decides the scope.

Who your customers are

If you have an existing site or a competitor you like, send them. It's the fastest way for me to understand your taste.

Your deadline

A firm date, a soft target, or "no rush" — knowing this changes what I recommend. I'll always tell you if the timeline is unrealistic.

Your content

Logo, images, text, prices, services. Have them ready or not — I can advise on what's needed either way.

Domain & hosting status

Do you already own a domain and hosting, or do you need that set up too? It affects the quote.

Your budget range

Even a rough range helps. I'd rather know early than quote something that doesn't work for you.
