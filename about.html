How I got here

I started building websites because I wanted things to exist online that didn't yet — tools that solved real problems for real people. What began as small pages for friends and small businesses grew into full platforms: hiring marketplaces where clients connect with workers, a corporate site for a materials supplier, and a publishing platform with categories and search.

Along the way I learned that the difference between a website that gets admired and one that gets paid for isn't decoration. It's speed, clarity and whether it works on the phone in someone's hand. Those became the rules I build every project by.

What I actually do

I take a business problem and turn it into something a customer can interact with. That ranges from a professional company site with a working enquiry form, to a React single-page app where people search and filter live listings. I'm involved end to end — planning the structure, writing the code, making it responsive, and deploying it so it stays online.

How I work with clients

I work in plain English and I say what I think. If a request won't help your business, you'll hear that before you pay for it. If something can't be done properly within a budget, I'll show you the realistic options instead of disappearing. Every project gets a written scope and a fixed price, so there are no surprises on the invoice.

After launch I'm still here. Websites need small updates — a new service, a corrected price, a photo swap. You shouldn't have to find a new developer for that, and you shouldn't be left waiting on someone who disappears.

What I'm looking for

I work best with founders and small teams who care about how their site feels and want it live quickly. If you need a website that makes you look established, a platform that lets customers do something useful, or an existing site untangled and made fast again, we'll get along.

My principles

Four things I won't compromise on

Clarity over cleverness

If a visitor has to think about what your site does, it's not finished. Plain language and obvious next steps win every time.

Speed is a feature

A site that loads quickly converts better and ranks better. Performance is part of the build, not an afterthought bolted on at the end.

Mobile is the main screen

Most of your customers will arrive on a phone. If it doesn't work in one hand, it doesn't work — and I design it that way from the first layout.

Say the honest thing

I'd rather decline a project than deliver something I'm not proud of. You'll get a straight answer, even when it costs me the work.

Own the outcome

Shipping is not handing over keys. I make sure it works on real devices and real screen sizes before I call it done.

Your content stays yours

Your copy, images, domain and accounts are all yours. I hand over a clean build with nothing proprietary locked inside it.
