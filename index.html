Good to know

Questions clients ask me

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — number of pages, whether you need a booking or enquiry system, and how much custom interactivity is required. Send me what you need and I'll give you a fixed quote with no hidden extras.

How long will it take?

A focused business site usually goes live within days, not weeks. Web apps with more logic take longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline before we start, and I'll tell you early if anything changes.

Can you work with the content I already have?

Yes. If you have an existing site, text, images or logos, I'll work with what you have and improve it. If you don't, I can write the copy structure and advise on the images you need.

Will I be able to update the site myself afterwards?

Yes. I'll hand it over clearly organised, and I'll show you how to make the everyday edits yourself. If you'd rather not touch it, I can handle updates for you.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. I work remotely with clients anywhere, across different time zones, with everything documented so we're never guessing about what's happening.
